## Typography

### Display / Headline Serif
**Cormorant Garamond** — Used for hero titles, pull quotes, and atmospheric text.
- Weights: 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 600 (SemiBold)
- Styles: Normal and Italic
- Character: Elegant, literary, cinematic. Evokes old manuscript and underground mystique.

### Heading Serif
**Playfair Display** — Used for section headers, article titles, tier names.
- Weights: 400, 700
- Character: Editorial authority, structured elegance.

### Body / UI Sans
**Inter** — Used for body text, navigation, labels, UI elements.
- Weights: 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium)
- Character: Clean, readable, modern. Contrasts the serif warmth without clashing.

## Logo System

### Primary Mark
- Symbol: Filled circle (●) in Archive Gold
- Wordmark: "Silent Cue" in Cormorant Garamond, letter-spacing 0.18em, uppercase
- The circle represents the cue ball. Its stillness. The moment before impact.

### Usage Rules
- Never use the logo on busy backgrounds without dark overlay
- Minimum clear space: 1× the height of the lettermark on all sides
- Do not add drop shadows
- Do not change the gold to any other color
- Acceptable: White wordmark with gold circle on dark backgrounds
- Acceptable: Gold wordmark + circle on black/charcoal

## Visual Language

### Photography / Imagery Direction
- **Low-key lighting**: pools of light in dark rooms. Single source, practical
- **Close details**: chalk on fingertips, cue tip, ball reflection, green felt texture
- **Silhouettes**: player silhouette against dim hall light
- **Slow motion feel**: blurred motion, long exposure, chalk dust mid-air
- **Neon reflections**: neon bar signs reflected in lacquered wood and ball surfaces
- **Smoke/fog**: light diffused through atmosphere
- **Never**: bright overhead sports photography, action shots, crowd shots

### Texture
- Film grain over all imagery (subtle, 3–5% opacity)
- Noise texture overlaid on all backgrounds
- Felt texture as a secondary background material
- Aged paper for print materials

### Motion Principles
- **Ease out** on all transitions (things decelerate, never bounce)
- **Slow reveals**: content fades up 0.8s minimum
- **No pop-ins**: nothing should appear suddenly
- **Parallax**: subtle depth on hero sections
- **Marquees**: slow (30–40s cycle), never urgent

## The "Hidden Archive" Interface Metaphor

The website should feel like accessing a private intelligence archive:
- Subdued navigation, not prominent
- Content revealed progressively as you scroll
- Locked content creates intrigue rather than frustration
- Typography should feel like it was typeset for private circulation
- Section numbers (I. II. III.) create the feel of a classified document
- Gold accents feel like ink, not neon

## Social Media Visual Direction

### Formats
- Square cards (1:1): Pull quotes with minimal text
- Vertical (9:16): Cinematic video content with narration
- Horizontal (16:9): YouTube/long-form thumbnail aesthetic

### Card Design System
- Background: `#0F0F0F` or `#161616`
- Text: Ivory on dark
- Gold accent: Single line, circle, or divider only
- No stock photo faces
- Imagery: Abstract textures, felt, chalk, close-detail pool photography
- Noise texture overlay always on

---

## Print / Ebook Aesthetic
- Paper: Off-white (`#F5F0E8`) with charcoal text
- Or: Black pages with ivory text (dark edition)
- Headers: Cormorant Garamond
- Body: Inter 9–10pt with generous leading (1.8)
- Chapter dividers: Horizontal line with roman numerals
- Pull quotes: Side-set in italic, gold rule
- Cover: Full bleed dark image, title in large Cormorant Garamond
