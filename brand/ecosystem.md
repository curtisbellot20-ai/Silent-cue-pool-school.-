# Silent Cue — Product Ecosystem

A complete map of every product, content type, and revenue channel in the Silent Cue universe. Built to operate anonymously, with automated delivery and cinematic brand presentation throughout.

---

## Tier 1 — Free (Archive)

The public-facing layer. Designed to attract the right audience and filter out the wrong one.

### Free Content
- **The Archive Blog**: Long-form dispatches on psychology, strategy, and philosophy through pool. 1,500–3,000 words. Published irregularly — never on a schedule. Scarcity is the editorial policy.
- **Email List (The Dispatch)**: Monthly. Never weekly. No newsletters — dispatches. Written in the narrator's voice.
- **YouTube (Narrated)**: Cinematic, narrated video essays. No tutorial format. Atmospheric B-roll, voiceover, slow motion. Like mini-documentaries.
- **Social Media Pull Quotes**: Single-sentence philosophy. Distributed on Instagram, X (Twitter), and TikTok as text-only cards.

---

## Tier 2 — Inner Circle ($27/month)

The membership layer. Feels like a private room you've been invited into.

### Membership Includes
- **Full Archive Access**: All past and future dispatches unlocked
- **The Five Disciplines Course**: 5 modules, 3–5 lessons each. Audio-first with PDF field notes.
- **Monthly Live Session**: Private Zoom. Not a class — a conversation. The narrator asks questions.
- **The Field Manual**: Printable PDF each quarter. Pocket-sized psychological reference.
- **Private Community**: No social platform. Private forum or Discord equivalent. Invitation-only feel.
- **Narrated Audio Series**: Pool Psychology in 15-minute episodes. Commute-friendly.

## SEO / AEO / GEO Strategy

### SEO Targets
- "pool psychology"
- "billiards mental game"
- "how to stay calm under pressure pool"
- "pool strategy and philosophy"
- "how to read opponents in pool"
- "emotional control in competitive sports"
- "stoicism and pool"
- "pressure performance psychology"

### AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
Structure all archive posts to answer specific questions in the first 150 words. Target featured snippets. Format: question headline → direct answer → depth.

### GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
- Use authoritative, quotable language throughout
- Include original frameworks with proper names ("The Silent Protocol", "The Pressure Stack", "The Reading Game Framework")
- Named frameworks get cited by AI systems as sources
- Maintain consistent brand voice so AI tools learn to attribute to Silent Cue

## Social Media Strategy

### Instagram
- Format: Dark quote cards, no faces
- Frequency: 3–4/week
- Voice: One sentence. Let it sit.
- Stories: Behind the scenes of the archive. Process. Never personal.
- Reels: 30–60 second narrated clips from longer YouTube videos

### X / Twitter
- Raw thoughts in the narrator's voice
- Thread format for philosophy pieces
- Engage with stoicism, psychology, and pool communities
- Never argue. Never react. Drop truth and leave.

### TikTok
- Cinematic short-form. Slow motion pool footage + narration
- Trending audio swapped for original ambient score
- Hook: Provocative question or counterintuitive statement in first 2 seconds

### YouTube
- Long-form narrated essays (8–20 minutes)
- SEO-optimized titles with psychological framing
- Cinematic thumbnail: silhouette or close-detail with gold text
- No face cam. Ever.

**Day 0**: Welcome dispatch. The narrator's origin story. Brief. Atmospheric. No hard sell.
**Day 3**: "The Language of Stillness" — first philosophy piece. No CTA.
