[Verse 1]
Eyes locked sideways on the edge of my face
Waitin' on pressure to leak from my frame
Didn't even study the balls on the cloth

Jaw got tight
Hands start sweatin'
Heart rate rise under quiet aggression
Then he buried me deep with a brutal safety
Cold little trap almost impossible lately

Cue ball frozen tight behind the rail line
No clean angle
No clear design
Then he leaned slow with a soft fake sigh
"Man… luck really ain't been on your side…"

That whisper hit hard like voltage through skin
Felt heat rush fast from the chest to the chin
Knuckles turned white on the wood too tight
Every nerve screamed "rush the shot right now!"
