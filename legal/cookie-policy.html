Cookie Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to site owners.

2. How Silent Cue Uses Cookies

Silent Cue currently does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies. We do not run ad networks or third-party tracking pixels on this site.

We use sessionStorage — a browser-side technology that is not a cookie — to store two small pieces of information during your visit:

  • Whether you have dismissed the intro overlay (so it does not repeat during your session)
  • Whether you have authenticated with an access code during the current session

SessionStorage data is stored entirely within your browser, is never transmitted to our servers, and is automatically cleared when you close your browser tab.

3. Third-Party Cookies

The following third-party services used on this site may set their own cookies:

  • Google Fonts — may log requests when loading typefaces. We do not control what Google collects. Refer to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Gumroad — when you click a purchase link, Gumroad may set cookies on their own domain. Refer to Gumroad's Privacy Policy.

4. Future Changes

If we add analytics, advertising, or other cookie-based services in the future, this policy will be updated and a cookie consent mechanism will be added to the site accordingly.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, delete, or block cookies from specific sites. Note that blocking cookies from third-party services like Gumroad may affect your ability to complete a purchase.
