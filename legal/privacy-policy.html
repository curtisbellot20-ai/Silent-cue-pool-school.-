Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Silent Cue ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Silent Cue website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our site and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the site and fulfill orders:

  • Email address — when you submit the email capture form on the homepage or sign up for dispatches.
  • Purchase information — handled entirely by Gumroad. We do not collect or store payment details. Gumroad's privacy policy governs that data.
  • Testimonial submissions — name or initials and written testimonial submitted via the Formspree form. Formspree's privacy policy governs form submission data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send you the free dispatch you requested
  • To notify you of new material, updates, or offers related to Silent Cue
  • To review and potentially publish testimonials with your permission

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies

Silent Cue does not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels. We use sessionStorage (a browser-side mechanism) to remember whether you have dismissed the intro overlay during your current session. This data is never sent to our servers and is cleared when you close your browser.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Gumroad — processes all purchases. Subject to Gumroad's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree — processes testimonial form submissions. Subject to Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — typefaces are loaded from Google's servers. Google may log request data per their standard policies.

5. Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you remain subscribed. You may unsubscribe at any time by replying to any dispatch with the word "unsubscribe" and your address will be removed within 7 days.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To make such a request, contact us at the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

Silent Cue is not directed at anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
